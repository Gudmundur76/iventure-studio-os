## Hidden Signals & Alpha
1. **The 351(k) Trigger**: Samsung Bioepis' expected filing this quarter will trigger the exchange of patent lists with Merck. This is the first public move in a multi-year litigation battle.
2. **Subcutaneous Diversion**: Merck is aggressively pushing US11633476B2 (expiration 2039) for its SC formulation. BIC recommends analyzing whether competitors have CMC capability for SC or are limited to IV.
3. **Medicare Impact**: Keytruda is a prime target for 2028 Medicare price negotiations under the Inflation Reduction Act (IRA). This could compress biosimilar margins earlier than expected.
