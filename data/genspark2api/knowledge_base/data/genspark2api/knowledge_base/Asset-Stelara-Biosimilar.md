# Asset Strategy: Stelara (Ustekinumab) Biosimilar
**Target Market:** US/Global
**Status:** Post-Patent Cliff (Price War Phase)

## Market Landscape (US 2026)
The US Stelara market transitioned to biosimilar competition in Jan 2025. As of Q2 2026, **8 biosimilars** have launched, including:
- [[Wezlana]] (Amgen) - **Interchangeable Status**.
- [[Selarsdi]] (Alvotech).
- [[Pyzchiva]] (Samsung Bioepis).
- [[Steqeyma]] (Celltrion).

## Intelligence Chain
- **Pricing Alpha**: Aggressive price erosion detected (Est. 40-60% discount vs Originator).
- **Interchangeable Advantage**: Amgen's Wezlana holds a structural moat at the pharmacy counter due to interchangeability.

## BIC Strategic Recommendation
- **For Manufacturers**: Focus on "Interchangeability" upgrades for late-stage pipeline assets to compete with Amgen.
- **For Payers**: Multi-source contracting recommended to maximize rebate pressure on J&J.
