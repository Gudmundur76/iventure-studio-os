# 5 Clinical Readouts to Watch in H1 2026 (BioSpace)
Source: https://www.biospace.com/drug-development/5-clinical-readouts-to-watch-in-h1-2026
Date: Jan 5, 2026

## Summary
The article highlights five major clinical trial readouts expected in the first half of 2026, covering cardiovascular disease, autoimmune conditions, Alzheimer's, wet AMD, and obesity.

## Key Catalysts
1. **Pelacarsen (Novartis/Ionis)**: Phase III Lp(a)HORIZON readout in H1 2026 for cardiovascular disease. Lowering Lp(a) is a major target for Big Pharma (Amgen, Lilly, Merck).
2. **Povetacicept (Vertex)**: Phase III RAINER interim data in H1 2026 for IgA nephropathy. Vertex aims for 2026 approval using a priority review voucher.
3. **Remternetug (Eli Lilly)**: Phase III TRAILRUNNER-ALZ 1 data expected in March 2026 for Alzheimer's. Follow-up to Kisunla.
4. **Axpaxli (Ocular Therapeutix)**: Phase III SOL-1 readout in Q1 2026 for wet AMD. Potential paradigm shift with 6-month dosing.
5. **WVE-007 (Wave Life Sciences)**: Multiple data readouts in Q1 and Q2 2026 for obesity. Focus on fat loss while preserving/increasing lean mass.

## Market Trends
- **Pipeline-in-a-product**: Leveraging one asset for multiple indications (e.g., povetacicept).
- **Muscle Preservation**: New obesity treatments focusing on body composition, not just weight loss.
