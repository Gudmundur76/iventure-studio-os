# Biosimilar Intelligence Company (BIC)
## Business Architecture & Strategy v1.0

BIC is a virtual intelligence hub that monetizes "Market Entry Alpha" for the global biosimilar sector.

### Core Value Proposition
Predicting patent expirations, regulatory success probabilities, and litigation outcomes to guide multi-million dollar clinical investments for physical manufacturers and private equity firms.

### Infrastructure Layers (The Stack)
1. **Brain**: [[VIC-Engine-v2]] (Wave 27+ live prediction loops).
2. **Knowledge**: [[knowledge_graph]] (Quantitative FDA/Patent metrics).
3. **Synthesis**: [[llm-wiki]] (Compounding market intelligence).
4. **Action**: [[Genspark-CLI]] (Real-time data ingestion via gsk search/crawl).

### Revenue Streams
- **Strategic Intelligence Reports**: 0k+ "Deep Dives" into US/EU patent cliffs.
- **Entry Window Optimization**: Advisory for timing CMC (Chemistry, Manufacturing, and Controls) scale-up.
- **Adversarial Audit**: Stress-testing manufacturer strategies using the [[RAAL]] layer.

### Initial Target
- [[Alvotech]] (Proven Pilot)
- US SMID-cap (Small/Mid-cap) Biotech developers.
